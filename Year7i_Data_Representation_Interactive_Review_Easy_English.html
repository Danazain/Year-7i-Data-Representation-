📘 Remember these words

Binary = 0 and 1.

Denary = numbers we use every day (0, 1, 2, 3...).

Hexadecimal (hex) = 0–9 and A–F.

ON/OFF values: 128 | 64 | 32 | 16 | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1
1 = ON • 0 = OFF

🔵 Round 1: Binary → Denary

Binary → Denary means 0s and 1s → normal number.
Step 1: Look at the 1s. Step 2: Add their values.
Example: 00001101 = 8 + 4 + 1 = 13.

🟢 Round 2: Denary → Binary

Denary → Binary means normal number → 0s and 1s.
Step 1: Choose numbers that add to your target. Step 2: Write 1 for ON and 0 for OFF.
Example: 10 = 8 + 2 = 00001010. Use 8 digits.

🟣 Round 3: Hexadecimal

Hex uses 0–9 and A–F.
A=10 • B=11 • C=12 • D=13 • E=14 • F=15.
Example: binary 1111 = 15 = hex F. Four binary digits = one hex digit.

🔥 Round 4: Expert challenge

Challenge: Use the same steps. For 8-bit binary → hex, split into two groups of 4.
Example: 1010 1111 → A F → AF.
